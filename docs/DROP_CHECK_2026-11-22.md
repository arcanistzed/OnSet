# Drop check: four tracks, yes or no

OnSet's sources disagree on where the **main drop** is in these four tracks, so this sheet asks
you directly. No musical training needed: you skip to a moment, listen, and answer yes or no.
About 10 minutes in all. Nothing in OnSet or rekordbox changes because of this sheet.

## What you're listening for

**The main drop** is the big moment of the track: after a build-up or a short pause, the full
beat, the bass and the main hook all hit together at full power. It's the moment a crowd reacts
to. It isn't simply the first time the drums come in, which can happen quietly near the start.

## How to listen

Use rekordbox (OnSet is closed while its data moves) or any player that shows the elapsed time.
Load the track, move the playhead to the **Start** time and play about 20 seconds. Each candidate
moment is about 10 seconds after its Start time, so you hear what leads into it.

For each moment, answer one question: **does the main drop hit here, give or take a second or
two?** Then, for each track, say which of its moments felt most like *the* drop, or "none" with
the time you heard it.

Volume at a normal listening level; headphones or speakers both work.
